Install the BA Day Calculator

Put it on your iPad or phone Home Screen. It works in store with no signal.

Scan to open on your iPad

igniteporcal.vercel.app
Open the calculator

iPad or iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open igniteporcal.vercel.app in Safari while you have signal.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open BA Day from your Home Screen. It now works offline.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open igniteporcal.vercel.app in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app at the bottom, or the ⋮ menu then Install app.

Updates arrive by themselves: open the app with signal now and then and it picks up the latest prices and maths.
